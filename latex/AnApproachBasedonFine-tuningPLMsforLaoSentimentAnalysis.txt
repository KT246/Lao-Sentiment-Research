3.3 Data Preprocessing and Cleaning Pipeline

To improve the quality of the Lao language dataset, several preprocessing and data filtering techniques were applied before training the final model.

First, the raw data was manually labeled to assign the correct sentiment class for each sentence. After labeling, the dataset underwent a data cleaning and preprocessing stage, where noise such as meaningless tokens, repeated characters, emojis, and invalid symbols were removed. Special attention was given to linguistic patterns in the Lao language, such as cases where vowels appear without consonants or where characters are combined in a way that does not produce meaningful words.

Next, a lightweight model was trained to quickly evaluate the dataset and identify potentially incorrect or low-quality samples. The model predictions were compared with the original labels to detect inconsistencies. Sentences that were correctly predicted and consistent with the labels were retained, while suspicious or incorrect samples were removed.

In addition, for extremely problematic samples, Cross-Validation using the K-Fold technique (K = 5) was applied. This approach helped identify unstable samples that were repeatedly misclassified across multiple folds. Such samples often contained invalid character patterns, including vowels without consonants or combinations that produced meaningless text. These samples were considered unreliable and removed from the dataset.

After filtering and validation, the remaining high-quality data was consolidated to create a Golden Dataset, which represents the most reliable subset of the data.

Finally, the golden dataset was split into training and validation sets to prepare for the final model training and evaluation.
